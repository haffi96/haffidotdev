The web and mobile platform behind EggMonkey, a community football group running weekly casual games, an 11-a-side league and tournaments across several UK cities.

Players browse fixtures on a calendar, join teams and book their spot, while memberships and pay-as-you-go bookings go through Stripe subscriptions and one-off payments. Match highlights are hosted and streamed with Mux. The Next.js web app is backed by a Hono API and Drizzle on Postgres, with Clerk handling auth, and a companion Expo (React Native) app shares the same backend.
